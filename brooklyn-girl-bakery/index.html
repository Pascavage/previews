About

Peri Anderson grew up baking in her mother's kitchen in Brooklyn. After seven years working her way from intern to pastry chef at the Night Kitchen Bakery in Chestnut Hill, she started Brooklyn Girl Bakery, where every cake is its own work of art with a little New York style. As she says, you can take the girl out of Brooklyn, but you can't take Brooklyn out of the girl.
