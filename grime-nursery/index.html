About

Paul and Bridget Grime bought this land from Paul's parents as a Vermont Christmas tree farm and grew it, one greenhouse at a time, into a six-greenhouse nursery. Today they grow over 100 varieties of trees, plus annuals, perennials and hanging baskets, and turn the shop into Santa's Workshop every winter.
