About

Pando's Pastry Shop opened on High Street in 2025 as a family business led by baker Brian Pando. The case is full of handmade pastries and cakes, from classic eclairs and cream puffs to tres leches and Nutella puffs, in both full-size and small portions that are perfect for events.
